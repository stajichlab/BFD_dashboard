results/annotated/reca_* and reca.treefile are from the RE-TRIMMED RecA/Rad51 tree (HPCC job 32026340, 327 alignment sites; the first tree with 41 sites was
discarded and replaced on 2026-11-11). Leave-one-out re-assigned 19 of 31 references: Dmc1 5/5 and Rad57 2/2 are usable; Rad55 (0/2) and Rad51 (0/7 under the strict
rule) are not. See ../RECA_RESULT.md before using any call.

results/rescue/ holds the rescue-layer ligase and Pol X tables and trees. Those trees used 13 (ligase) and 65 (Pol X) alignment columns, so they are untested
at tree level, not evidence about the rescued models (README, "What the rescue layer added").
